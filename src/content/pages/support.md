RadioWBF is made by volunteers: artists, designers, social workers and activists who open the studio for anyone who wants to be heard. There are no ads and no sponsors telling us what to play.

## Why your support matters

**Keep the signal on.** Your support covers streaming, equipment and the studio.

**Stay independent.** It keeps RadioWBF a free space for music, stories and conversations that do not fit anywhere else.

**Open the doors wider.** It helps us invite new voices through the open call and our workshops.

Thank you for listening, sharing and being part of the RadioWBF community.
