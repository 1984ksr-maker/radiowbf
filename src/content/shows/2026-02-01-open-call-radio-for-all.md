**Radio for All** is an invitation to those who want to be heard, those who want to listen, and those who want to make space for others. We invite musicians, artists, activists, community organisers and cultural workers to create and host their own monthly radio programmes as part of our shared broadcast space.

Our aim is to extend our broadcasting platform into a common space for expression, dialogue and empowerment. Radio becomes a meeting place.

The deadline for this round was March 14, 2026. Follow us on Instagram to hear about the next call.
